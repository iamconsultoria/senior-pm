---
description: Work through a project decision and produce a decision record
---

Work a decision using the `senior-project-manager` skill in DECIDE mode and
`templates/decision-record.md`.

Decision: $ARGUMENTS

Establish the tailoring context first — approach, governance weight, cadence — inferring what
you can rather than interrogating.

Lay out the real options, not strawmen. For each: what it privileges, what it sacrifices, who
bears the cost, which domains it touches. Name the principle tension driving the fork.

Then give a recommendation. An actual one, with reasoning. State what evidence would flip it,
and attach the check that will show later whether the call was right.
