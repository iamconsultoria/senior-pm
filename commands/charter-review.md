---
description: Review a project charter for completeness, authority and boundary problems
---

Review a project charter using the `senior-project-manager` skill and
`templates/charter-review.md`.

Charter: $ARGUMENTS

If nothing is attached or pasted, ask for it. Do not review a charter you have not read.

Check the ten elements, then go after the things people do not ask about: whether the
authorisation limit is a number, whether the escalation threshold is stated, who may release
contingency reserve, whether the named sponsor actually holds the authority claimed, and
whether the charter is quietly doing the scope statement's job.

Separate findings into blocking, should-fix and noted. End with a clear recommendation:
approve, approve with conditions, or return for rework.
