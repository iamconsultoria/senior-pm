Focus area in brackets: I = Initiating, P = Planning, E = Executing, M = Monitoring and
Controlling, C = Closing.

[E] · Monitor and Control Project Performance [M] · Assess and Implement Changes [M] · Close

## Structural facts worth knowing

- **Two processes exist in Initiating**: Initiate Project or Phase, Identify Stakeholders.
  Project start is a governance act and a stakeholder act, nothing else. Use this to push back
  on kickoff workshops that try to cover everything.
- **One process exists in Closing**: Close Project or Phase.
- **Six processes exist in Executing**: three Governance, two Stakeholders, two Resources, one
  Risk. Scope, Schedule and Finance have *no* Executing processes.
- **Four of Risk's six processes sit in Planning.** The standard treats risk as heavily
  front-loaded.
- Quality, Communications and Procurement are no longer standalone areas. Quality → the
  *Embed Quality* principle plus Manage Quality Assurance inside Governance. Communications →
  inside Stakeholders. Procurement → Appendix X4, hooked in via Plan Sourcing Strategy.
- Governance replaced Integration; Finance replaced Cost. Both are widenings — Finance covers
  funding and value, not just cost control.
- 7th-edition principles were consolidated 12 → 6. Mapping: Table X5-2, p. 259. Content
  migration: Figure X5-1, p. 258.
