# Artifacts — Inputs and Outputs (§4, pp. 113–144)

~104 entries, alphabetical. Grouped below by what they are for. The guide is explicit that the
per-process I/O lists are **illustrative, not comprehensive** `→ Guide §2, p. 8` — never
present them as mandatory.

## Authorising and business documents
Project charter · business case · business documents · benefits management plan ·
organizational capabilities · regulations

The **business case** is the economic feasibility study establishing validity of the benefits —
costs, benefits, how value is created, success criteria. Everything traces here. If a
deliverable does not, ask why it exists.

## Plans (the project management plan and its components)
Project management plan (+updates) · scope management plan · requirements management plan ·
schedule management plan · financial management plan · quality management plan · resource
management plan · communications management plan (+updates) · stakeholder engagement plan ·
risk management plan · procurement management plan · change management plan · configuration
management plan · development approach · sourcing strategy plan · funding strategy

Each domain contributes a component; Integrate and Align Project Plans consolidates them →
`04-governance.md`.

## Baselines
Scope baseline · schedule baseline (+updates) · cost baseline (+updates) · **performance
measurement baseline (PMB)**

The PMB is the integrated scope-schedule-cost baseline against which EVM is measured. Cost
baseline and **project funding requirements** are different lines `→ Figure 4-1, p. 126`.

## Scope and requirements artifacts
Requirements documentation · requirements traceability matrix `Figure 4-3, p. 132` · project
scope statement `Table 4-1, p. 129 compares it with the charter` · work breakdown structure
(WBS) · WBS dictionary · work package estimation · user stories · deliverable · verified
deliverables · final product, service, or result transition

**Verified deliverables** come out of Monitor and Control Scope and feed into Validate Scope.
Verification is internal; validation is the customer accepting. Keep them distinct.

## Financial artifacts
Cost estimates · basis of estimates · project funding requirements · funding proposals ·
revenue forecasts · cost forecasts

**Basis of estimates** is the one people skip. An estimate without its assumptions cannot be
defended when challenged or updated when conditions change — it can only be argued about.

## Risk artifacts
Risk register (+updates) · risk report · risk breakdown structure `Figure 4-5, p. 135` ·
probability and impact definitions `Table 4-2, p. 136`

Register = individual risks. **Report = overall project risk exposure.** Most organisations
maintain the first and never produce the second, which is why nobody can answer "how risky is
this project overall?"

## Performance data — the three-tier distinction
**Work performance data** → raw observations and measurements
**Work performance information** → data analysed in context
**Work performance reports** → information packaged for a decision

Confusing these is why status reports are long and useless. A steering pack full of data rather
than information forces the committee to do the analysis, and they will not.
