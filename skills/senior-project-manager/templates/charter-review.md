## Authorisation check
- Is the authority limit a number? An unbounded charter delegates nothing.
- Is the escalation threshold stated — where a decision stops being the PM's?
- Who may release contingency reserve, on what evidence?
- Does the named sponsor actually hold the authority claimed?
